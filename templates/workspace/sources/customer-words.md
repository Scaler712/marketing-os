# customer words

paste exact words from real people. do not tidy, summarise or fix grammar: their words
are the data. remove names, emails, phone numbers and anything else identifying.

add new entries at the bottom with the next id. never edit or delete an old entry.

entry format:

## Q001-EXAMPLE
- added: YYYY-MM-DD
- source: what kind of source, plus something you can go back to (file, url, timestamp)
- conversation_id: a non-identifying id, so one conversation is not counted twice
- public_use_permission: yes / no / unknown (no and unknown are research only)
- who_said_it: bought / asked but did not buy / asked for a refund
- said_on: YYYY-MM-DD or unknown
- words: "exact words here"
- tags: pain / tried-and-failed / wanted-outcome / objection / wrong-belief / phrasing
