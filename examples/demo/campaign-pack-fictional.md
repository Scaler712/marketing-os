## landing page copy, section by section

1. headline: know what you owe before the deadline, not after it
2. the problem, in buyer words: you have not touched the books since last spring, and the spreadsheet died in march
3. what you get: twelve months of bookkeeping caught up and filed, plus one video walkthrough of the result
4. proof: eleven written reviews, used with permission
5. biggest worry and the answer: "what if my year is too messy" - if we cannot catch up your year, we refund the 240 EUR in full
6. price and terms, word for word from the brief: 240 EUR once, paid up front
7. one button: catch up my year - 240 EUR

the page headline repeats the ad headline almost word for word on purpose. a person who
clicked has to see what they were promised.

## three organic posts

1. the three-days-before-the-deadline moment, told without selling
2. why the spreadsheet dies in march
3. what "caught up" actually means, step by step

## one follow-up email

for people who reached the page and did not buy: repeat the promise, answer the "too
messy" worry with the refund wording, one link back, no new claim.
